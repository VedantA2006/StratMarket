Reading the results

What the numbers support, and what to treat with caution. Added commentary, not part of the engine output.

The test windows are very short.The four M15 strategies cover about 11 weeks and 26 to 46 trades each. Eight or fewer losing trades per strategy cannot separate skill from a favourable stretch of gold. The 1H strategy has 58 trades over about 21 months, which is better but still thin.
Several figures are internally inconsistent.Profit factor is identical in every regime, month and long/short split of a given strategy (for example 6.11 everywhere for Grandmaster), which real trade lists almost never produce. The 1H Sovereign shows a max loss streak of 1 and $14 losers, yet a $149.19 max drawdown, which a closed-trade history cannot generate. It also reports 1 weekend hold while describing itself as flat over weekends. Check these against the raw trade list before trusting any metric.
Robustness tests look too clean.A 1-bar execution delay changes nothing in any strategy, spread sensitivity moves profit in perfectly straight lines, and every Monte Carlo run is profitable. Sharpe ratios of 5.8 to 10.7 are far above what live strategies deliver. These are signs the tests are not stressing the strategy, not signs of safety.
Results appear selected, not discovered.Each is labelled "filtered from 40 candidates", and the names advertise win-rate targets (88.5%, 80%+, 74-75%). Strategy 3 even notes that October was "boosted" to +10R. Parameters like 2.35, 2.5 and offsets of $3, $4, $5 above EMA200 look tuned to this window. Selection from many candidates inflates the winner's result, and the claim of "zero overfitting" rests on an unspecified walk-forward split.
Position sizing does not match the stated 1% risk.Net profit is only 0.33% to 0.85% on $100,000, and every winner or loser is exactly $14 to $20. That looks like 1 oz per trade rather than 1% risk. At true 1% risk Grandmaster's +40.9R would be roughly +41%, with larger drawdowns. Confirm the sizing before comparing returns.
Before relying on any of them.Test on data the optimiser never saw (a different year of gold), re-run with realistic broker costs and commissions, confirm the trade list reconciles, and check how each parameter behaves when nudged. Win rates near 80% with a payoff near 1.2 mean a few extra losses change results quickly: breakeven is 41% to 45%, but a drop from 88% to 70% erases most of the edge.
